ARES Battery Guardian 2.0.0 Beta 1

This is a BETA release. Please read before installing.

What ARES does
- Monitors your battery and charger, records history, and warns you (alarm, notification and
  an optional full-screen reminder) when the battery reaches the limits you set.
- ARES itself never switches charging off. Where a documented BIOS charge setting is available
  (for example Dell Custom Charge, which needs administrator rights), ARES can ask the firmware to
  use your limits; the laptop firmware, not ARES, then controls charging. Otherwise you unplug
  the charger when ARES tells you to. The charging state shown always comes from Windows
  battery telemetry.

Experimental
- Smart Wake-Up Protection is EXPERIMENTAL. It has not yet been confirmed on any laptop
  that Windows wakes the computer from Sleep to run the battery check. It is off unless you
  turned it on.

Optional: Smart Audio Override (off by default)
- While a protection alarm sounds, ARES can un-mute Windows and set the volume to 100%, then
  restore your volume and mute setting. If you change the volume during the alarm, your choice is
  kept. Turn it on in Settings > D.

Battery History hover details
- Move the mouse over the History chart to see the nearest recorded point: exact battery
  percentage, time, charging or discharging, and voltage and capacity when the battery driver
  recorded them.

Hardware Diagnostics
- On a PC without supported firmware charge control, the Dashboard no longer shows the long
  compatibility notice. It says "Not available on this PC (monitoring only)" in the Charge
  control card. The full details are in Settings > I. Hardware Diagnostics.

Your data is kept
- Settings, battery history, sessions, events, custom alarm-sound paths and the database are
  stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ARES Battery Guardian. Installing, updating, uninstalling and
  reinstalling never delete or overwrite that folder.

Installation
- Installs for the current Windows user only and does not need administrator rights.
- Program folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\ARES Battery Guardian
- Start at Windows sign-in is controlled by the "Start with Windows" setting inside ARES
  (on by default); an existing choice is kept.
- Uninstalling removes the program, its shortcuts, the Windows sign-in entry and the Smart
  Wake-Up task that point at this copy. Your data folder is left in place; delete it yourself
  if you no longer want the history.
